Terms & Conditions

SmartCampus Complaint Redressal System — Department of MCA, SIT Mangalore

1. Purpose

SmartCampus is an academic project built to help students report genuine hostel and campus issues (electrical, plumbing, wifi, food, and other concerns) so they can be routed to the right department and tracked to resolution. It is not an official, institution-operated grievance system unless formally adopted by the college administration.

2. Account Responsibility

You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential. Do not share your account with others. You are responsible for all activity that happens under your account, including complaints submitted using it.

3. Honest Use Only

Complaints must describe genuine issues you have personally experienced or observed on campus. Submitting false, exaggerated, or fabricated complaints is a misuse of this system and may result in your complaint being marked as spam and your account being suspended.

4. Automated Classification

Complaint category and priority are predicted automatically using a machine learning model and keyword-based rules. These predictions are estimates to help route your complaint faster — they are not guaranteed to be perfectly accurate, and department staff may recategorize or reprioritize a complaint on review.

5. Rate Limits & Anti-Abuse Measures

To keep the system usable for everyone, submissions are limited to a reasonable number per day, with a short cooldown between submissions, and near-duplicate complaints may be blocked. These limits exist to prevent flooding and are not intended to discourage reporting real, distinct issues.

6. Administrator Actions

Administrators can view, update the status of, flag as spam, hide, or permanently delete complaints (deletion and hiding are only available once a complaint is marked Resolved). Administrators may suspend accounts found to be submitting spam or abusive content. Suspended accounts lose the ability to log in until reinstated.

7. Data Collected

The system stores your name, email, room number, and the complaints you submit, strictly to operate the complaint-routing and tracking features described above. Your password is stored as a one-way hash and is never visible to administrators or anyone else.

8. No Warranty

This is a student academic project. It is provided "as is" for demonstration and educational purposes, without warranty of uptime, accuracy, or fitness for handling real emergencies. For urgent safety issues (fire, electrical hazards, medical emergencies), contact hostel wardens or emergency services directly rather than relying solely on this system.

9. Changes

These terms may be updated as the project evolves. Continued use of the system after changes are posted means you accept the updated terms.
